# PureRootFood

**Know what's in your food. Find clean sources.**

PureRootFood is an iOS app that helps you understand the ingredients in packaged foods and connects you with farms, butchers, and clean-food shippers across the US.

## Features

- **Ingredient scanner** with a curated 45-ingredient database, IARC carcinogen flagging, and NOVA processing scores
- **Barcode lookup** powered by the Open Food Facts database
- **12 hand-vetted nationwide organic shippers**
- **8 featured small farms** across the US
- **Trusted local food directory** linking to LocalHarvest, USDA, EatWild, Real Milk Finder, and more

## Pricing

PureRootFood is completely free — no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads.
